# Beta 1.7.21 graphics

Walls use [Concrete Wall 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001) by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, CC0-1.0. Original 2K PNGs and SHA-256 provenance are in [ConcreteWall001](../Tools/PBRSource/ConcreteWall001/SOURCE.json). Base colour is sRGB; the DirectX normal uses normal compression; ARM is linear masks (R occlusion, G roughness, B metallic). Three streamed textures use mipmaps and a 2048 maximum. The source scan is 150 cm wide.

[build_wall.py](../Tools/build_wall.py) builds the wall material with Unreal's world-aligned texture/normal functions. The normal function explicitly enables `WorldSpace` output to match the material's disabled tangent-space-normal setting; UE 5.4 defaults that function switch to false. This prevents back-facing walls from receiving incorrect direct sunlight. Texture size remains consistent when wall segments are rotated or stretched. The existing wall material instance is retained and receives the new parent. Meshes, collision and obstacle placement are unchanged. Regular setup builds this material after obstacle import; subsequent targeted wall imports preserve its parent.

The existing daylight map uses three shadow cascades over 120 m, shadow bias 0.35 and skylight intensity 0.8 (previously 1.05). Runtime post-processing adds world-space SSAO with 70 cm radius, intensity 0.85 and power 1.4. These are authored visual settings awaiting gameplay acceptance. The saved AO toggle remains authoritative even on Low post-processing; choosing any preset enables AO, while loading an existing profile preserves its choice. Screen-space contact shadows are disabled at every quality level after Level 12 testing exposed patterned false wall shadows. Cascaded shadow maps and SSAO retain the proximity cues. High texture quality uses 16x anisotropy, Medium 8x and Low 4x.

DX11/SM5, conventional shadow maps and the existing texture pool remain. No Lumen, ray tracing, mesh distance fields, displacement or new geometry. AO is a screen-space effect: it cannot provide off-screen occlusion or a metric wall-distance guarantee. Their cost on the minimum GPU has not been measured.

Asset generation and validation outcomes are recorded in [knowledge-graph.md](../knowledge-graph.md). After rebuilding on another checkout, run regular `SetupProject.cmd` with Unreal closed to regenerate assets from the included source maps. Check both cameras close to walls, normal orientation on opposite faces, the AO switch at Low/Medium, shadow stability and frame time on target hardware.
